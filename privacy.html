Privacy Policy

Updated November 18, 2026.

Purpose and access

LAA Research Drive Sync is a personal research utility operated by its owner. Using rclone and Google OAuth, it may access file names, metadata and contents and create, update, move or delete Google Drive files when directed by the owner.

Use and storage

Google Drive data is used for owner-requested file synchronization, backup, transfer and integrity verification. Files are stored in owner-selected research systems and Google Drive locations. OAuth credentials are stored in the owner's rclone configuration. This public website does not collect Google Drive files or OAuth tokens.

Sharing

The utility does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Any sharing of files depends on the owner's configured Google Drive permissions and research collaborations.

Retention and deletion

The owner controls the retention and deletion of transferred files. OAuth access can be revoked through Google Account settings, and local rclone credentials can be deleted. Revocation does not automatically delete files previously transferred.

Contact

Questions may be submitted via the repository's public Issues page; do not post private data or credentials there.
